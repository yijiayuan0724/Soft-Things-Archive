The Thanksgiving Twins

Thanksgiving, 2021

My boyfriend meant to send me one big bunny for Thanksgiving in 2021. He accidentally used his billing address as the shipping address, so the bunny arrived at his own home. He ordered another one for me, and that is how we ended up with two. A little address mix-up became a pair we could share.

Blue Bunny

A birthday gift at fourteen

My best friend from middle school gave me this bunny for my fourteenth birthday, before I left China to attend high school in the United States. I gave her a stuffed animal too, so we each had a soft friend to keep. This bunny reminds me of her and the friendship I carried with me when I moved away.

My Named Bunny

The beginning of us

My boyfriend gave me this bunny when we first got together. It has my name on it, which makes it feel especially mine. It brings me back to those early days of our relationship and the small gestures that made them special.

Letter Bunny

A little messenger

This bunny carries a letter my boyfriend wrote to me. I think it was a Valentine’s Day gift. The occasion is a little blurry, but the letter is what makes this bunny special: a soft little keeper of his words.
